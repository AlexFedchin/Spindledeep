Spindledeep · changes of 26 November, third batch

Rolling On

The bobbins pay the thread no mindthey roll over you and away

The cart-sized bobbins in the Treadles and below no longer jam on your thread. They roll up and down their lanes without stopping, and the way past is to lie in a gutter and let one roll over you.

The Bobbins

make the bobbins roll on past me instead of stopping, they should ignore the thread

  • Gone: jamming on thread lying in the lane, the 4.2 s stop, the 4 m of yarn it cost, its sound, the shudder, and the red glow on a stuck bobbin.
  • Unchanged: the lanes, the speeds, the gutters, and being crushed if one hits you standing up.
  • The tip in the first lane now reads “lie low in the gutter and let it roll over you”.
