Water and Words

The falls still dripand every seat has something to say

A second pass over the two new stretches and the end of the game. The Dry Falls' seat now shows the whole cave and has its words, water drips and the walls sweat. The Underway has things to come upon. The Stores are less than half as long, with things in the way. Every seat has a line. The Loom can't be skipped by hanging on the thread, the figures on its floor are drawn by hand, and the bitten ending plays in the order you asked for. She bends her knees when she sits.

The Stores, shorter

The storage is also waaay too long and boring, I just walk in zig zag. Add some more obstacles and make sure that each pillow has some world story shown.

Five galleries became two, and the walk is less than half as long. The bot now crosses it in 37 s instead of 59 s, for about 140 m of walking instead of 330. The hall is 76 m long instead of 86.

  • Crates on the top gallery to hop.
  • A gap with a ring over it, the same reach as the Bell Gallery's. Miss it and you land in a bin below with a rope back up, so there is no way round it and nothing is lost.
  • A wall of bales on the second gallery, four metres high, with a rope up its face and a seat on top.
  • A hole in the floor of the gallery to drop through to the way out. Jump over it instead to reach the Drying Loft.
  • Both rope climbs and the ring were played by the bot: through the gap, and out of the bin after a miss.

No shortcut in the Loom

I have also found a way to cheat the loom: if I hang on the thread while falling into it I can climb on like the middle of the way up already. please fix it.

From the ledge where you come into the Loom down to the cloth, until you have tied the pole on the cloth, holding the thread does nothing and reeling in does nothing. If you were already hanging on it, you let go. Tested by running off the ledge holding the thread and reeling up:

The floor of the Loom

And I don't like the pictures of women in the bottom of the loom. Please either make them by hand or come up with something better there, I don't even understand what they mean.

The photographs are gone, and the figures are drawn by hand in the game's own line. Each one is the nightclothes someone wore, standing upright with nobody inside: a long nightgown, a nightshirt over trousers, a gown with a shawl, or a coat thrown over. There is no head and no hands. The sleeves hang empty and the hems sway. When one turns toward her, the collar gapes open, long and black. They still turn, follow her and shake when the dark wakes, as before.

What they areYour question, answered. Story.

Yes: they are the called, everyone it called down by name over the years, standing where they came to on the floor of the Loom. Drawn as only their nightclothes, it reads as people taken from their beds, with nothing of them left inside.

The bitten ending

For the thread biting ending: first show the main cutscene, then texts on black screen and then the last cutscene. Only then the titles go. And after the monster tries to climb out and the doll is walking to the candle - make it sit on something, right now it sits on nothing. And the monster's head and neck should be behind the doll, not over it.

  • In order. Its film, then the cards on black, then the last film, then the titles, then back to the title screen. Holding to skip the cards goes straight to the last film; skipping the titles goes to the title screen.
  • Something to sit on. Her knitting basket now stands by the candle, shut, with a needle out from under the lid. The doll walks to it and sits on top, knees bent and feet hanging.
  • Behind her. For this scene the doll, the candle and what is around them are drawn over the thing, so its neck and head pass behind her.
