The firewood under the lean-to is now a loose heap. Every log rests on the ground or on the logs beneath it, so they all touch, and no two sit in a neat row.

The woodshed

I don't like the firewood in the cabin, it's too orderly. Make it more chaotic, and make the logs actually touch each other.

BeforeAfter

Drag the thread to compare

  • Dropped, not placed. When the game starts, it drops about thirty logs into the shed one at a time. Each one falls until it hits the ground or another log. If it lands balanced on a single log, it rolls off to one side until two logs cradle it. The result is a heap where every log touches its neighbours, and it comes out the same every time you play.
  • Different sizes and cuts. Logs vary from 20 to 40 cm across. About a third are split, as halves or quarters, so the heap has wedges and flat faces as well as rounds.
  • Each end is drawn as real wood. Each log has a dark bark ring, off-centre growth rings and a drying crack. Each log also sits at its own angle and is slightly squashed, so no two read the same.
  • Snow on top. The logs at the top of the heap carry a little snow blown in under the eave.
  • A chopping block by the shed. A stump with its rings showing stands in the snow, with her axe left bitten into it and a few split pieces and chips around it.

Where it is

  • The heap is built by makeWoodpile and drawn by drawLogEnd, at the bottom of app/src/draw.js. Changing the number 913 in it gives a different heap.
