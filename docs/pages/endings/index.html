Spindledeep · the two ending films

Both Ends

the thaw, the chairand the way down

Both ending films have been staged again by the rules of the new opening film. Hands rest on what they hold, feet stay where they are put, every camera runs straight at one speed, and each sound is placed by the movement that makes it. The bitten ending is 45 seconds long and the tied one 56. The tied ending was made of drawn plates and is now animated throughout. It stays sealed near the bottom of this page.

The camera

All twelve moves run in a straight line at one speed from the first frame to the last. Within any shot the fastest and slowest frames differ by at most 0.007 mm. Over snow and grass the cameras track sideways, so the engraved lines stand still on the ground.

The dark

Where no light falls, no line is drawn any more. Before, figures standing in the dark were outlined and could be seen before they should be. This is in the grading step, so it applies to any film made from now on.

The sound

  • 395 sounds are written by the movement. Each clod of snow, drop, runner, footfall and hand notes its own frame, and the moment of contact in the recording is laid on it.
  • Checked in the finished files: in both films the mixed sound sits 0.0 ms from where the movements put it, in every stretch loud enough to measure. Sounds that ring out alone (runners, boards, drops, hands, the lamp) start within 2 ms of their frame. Many of the softer ones overlap and cannot be timed one by one.
  • No magic sounds in either film. The tied ending has no music at all.
