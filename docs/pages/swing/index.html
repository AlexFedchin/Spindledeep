A Heavier Swing

A small doll on a long threadslow to start, slow to fall

Swinging on a ring is now slower and gentler, and it builds up over a swing or two instead of flinging you across. Every ring gap in the game was re-checked with the game's own physics, and three rings and two islands in the Ink Mere moved a little so each gap still works.

This is a feel change, so there are no screenshots. The charts below come straight from the game's physics.

What changed

The swinging on the rope feels too quick and intense. The doll is weak: it should take some time to start swinging properly, and it should be slower.

  • The catch soaks up her speed. When the thread snags a ring, the doll keeps only a quarter of the speed she arrived with, and the thread gives a little. She dips under the ring and starts from almost nothing.
  • The arc is slower. One full swing back and forth on a ring now takes about 3.1 seconds, up from 2.2. Her top speed while just hanging is 5.5 m/s, down from 9.1.
  • She builds height by leaning in. Pressing the direction she's already swinging adds height. Pressing against the swing does very little, so you can't yank yourself around any more. Holding the direction you want to go is enough: she gains a little on every forward swing.
  • Letting go is gentler. The fastest she can leave a ring is 9 m/s, down from 10.
  • A ring no longer slips off. Before, swinging back far enough could pull the thread back out of the ring and drop you. The thread now stays through the ring until you let go. This only showed up once swings got slower and went back and forth more.

Leaning into every swing

Before, leaning in whipped the doll around the ring about every second, at up to 22.7 m/s. Now each swing takes about two and a half seconds and peaks at 13.8 m/s. The first forward swing still gets you across if you time the let-go, so you aren't forced to pump, but it no longer happens the instant you catch.

Every gap still works

Each gap was simulated with a run-up, a jump, a catch and a let-go, at every let-go time from 0.1 to 6 seconds. It was run twice, once holding grip from the jump and once pressing it at the ring, and once holding forward and once leaning with each swing. Every gap has several let-go windows in all four styles. To keep the Ink Mere gaps working with the gentler swing:

  • Island 1 is 0.9 m wider on its east side, and island 3 is 0.7 m wider on its east side.
  • The first Mere ring moved 0.4 m back toward the shore and 0.7 m up. The island 2 → 3 ring moved 0.7 m right and 0.3 m up.
  • The last Gallery ring, before the east ledge, moved 0.5 m left and 1.2 m up, so the doll swings over the ledge's lip instead of under it.
