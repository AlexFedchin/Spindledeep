A longer way down

make the map bigger without adding new mechanics or dangers […] It should feel like a lonely descent but I am only listening to notes all the time and reading letters […] The new parts of the map should come to around +5 minutes of gameplay.

The world is twice as wide below ground now. Everything that was there is where it was; the new caves are cut into fresh rock east of the old edge, and the way down passes through them twice. Nothing in them can hurt you: no ink, thorns, hands, machines or moths, and no rings or ropes are needed. Every drop is a step down and no fall does harm. There are no new knots, letters or lines at the seats. Each stretch has a quiet room with a seat, its own backdrop painting, lamps, things left lying about and its own sound.

The Dry Falls

The shaft under the veil no longer drops straight into the Mere. Its foot opens onto a dry stream bed that runs east into a tall cave of stone basins, seven of them stepping down, each with a worn rim where water once stood and stone curtains hanging far overhead. A chute at the far end drops into the Cistern, a round quiet room with a seat. From there the Underway, a long low gallery, runs about a hundred metres back west under the basins and comes out on the Ink Mere's east shore. Poles stand at the foot of the shaft, at the lowest basins, in the Cistern and halfway along the Underway.

The Stores

The heddle corridor no longer ends at the well: it runs on east into a storehouse hall cut in the rock, sixty metres tall and eighty-five long, with five galleries one under another. You go east along the top, step off its end onto the next, go back west along that one, and so on down, five floors, then west out of the bottom to the well's old ledge. The well itself is filled in. Shelves of great empty bobbins stand on every gallery, timber posts hold them up, and bales of wool hang on chains between them. Off the third gallery's west end is the Drying Loft, a small quiet room with skeins hung over poles, candles and a seat.

Voice notes wait their turn

also I sometimes reach next voice note before the old one finished playing. Then there are notes overlapping with them.

  • One at a time. A knot you reach while she is still speaking, or while a letter is open, waits. It starts 1.8 seconds after the last one has finished, or after the letter is put away. A seat's line waits for her voice too.
  • Further apart. The Dry Falls lies between her knot in the Bell Gallery and the one in the Ink Mere, and the Stores between the Treadles' knot and the lower room's. One letter on the Bell Gallery's crowded east ledge (you found two there twelve seconds apart) now lies in the Cistern. Nothing new was written.
