The mushrooms

They were the mushroom pots of the Seed Hollow, the hidden room under the Root Gardens. Things are set down by looking for the floor below them, but in the hidden rooms that search began inside the roof and climbed out of it upwards. Ten things ended up 5 to 14 metres above the rooms they belong to, nine of them inside solid rock: the pots, a row of garden markers, a lantern, a bucket, candles, a stool, letters, boots and two piles of baskets. All ten are now in their rooms and stay hidden until the room is found.

Everything else in the rock

I then drew every layer of the game over the whole world and listed each spot where something shows inside rock. The list went from 192 entries, one for each two-metre square, to 11. Those 11 are meant: the ends of the roots and yarn the upper shelves hang by, a corner of a basket on a slope, and the glow of two candles on the floor under them.

  • Warp threads in the Loom. The hundreds of threads behind everything ran straight through ledges and up to 13 metres into the walls. They now stop at the rock.
  • Heddles and needles. The heddles of the Treadles were drawn over the ceiling when pulled up, and the Loom’s needles showed through the wall. Both now come out of the rock.
  • The woodshed. Its back reached half a metre down through the snow as a dark slab.
  • Blots in the ledges. In the middle of every ledge the engraved lines clotted into small white squiggles that looked like things in the ground. The lines now run level there.
  • Dark shapes in front. Mounds, hanging roots and stalactites in the foreground drifted over the rock as you moved, or hung from nothing. The mounds are gone, the grass stays on the floor, and the rest shows only where it hangs from a roof.
  • Two smaller ones. The veil over the shaft in the Bell Gallery ends at the shaft’s walls, and the old faded thread in the Treadles lies on the ground instead of cutting through it.

What lies there on purpose

About a hundred things are set into the rock over the whole descent, never two within eight metres of each other. They are cut into the rock’s own lines, so they take the same light, fade with depth the same way, and are not there at all in the dark. You will pass most of them without noticing.

  • They follow the depth. Shells and roots near the top, then small beasts and broken pots, fish under the Mere, and worse further down.
  • They are always well inside the rock. Every part of each one is checked against the cave, and none is near a hidden room, on an upper shelf, or under the top of the Loom.
