## Images
Backdrop paintings generated locally with Stable Diffusion XL 1.0 and RealVisXL V5.0
(CreativeML Open RAIL++-M), depth-split with Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0),
and re-engraved at runtime by the game's renderer. All other art is procedural.

## Voice
The knitter: Qwen3-TTS 12Hz 1.7B VoiceDesign (Alibaba Qwen, Apache-2.0), takes scored with Whisper large-v3-turbo (MIT).
